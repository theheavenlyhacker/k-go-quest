The life of an Attempt, across the tablet/server boundary

The question this answers. K-Go Quests grades some Content Packs on the tablet and some only on the server. Three rules are undecided, and all three bite the same object — one Learner's answer to one Exercise, on its way from a tablet that may be offline for a year to a server that has the last word:

  1. Who owns Mastery — the tablet's estimate, or the server's?
  2. What happens to an answer older than the server's 30-day upload window?
  3. Is there one wallet of Coins or two?

Each is a switch below. Press the buttons, change a switch, and watch what it does to a Learner who has already practised. The cases worth watching are in the walkthroughs: they're the ones that look fine on paper.
